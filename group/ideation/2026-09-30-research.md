# KBC Autopilot: research notes (30 Sep 2026)

Compiled with research agents from public sources. Use "not visible to customers today" in the pitch; KBC may have internal tooling we can't see.

## What Kate already does

- About 140 proactive situations in Belgium (duplicate payment, low balance, service vouchers running low, card not activated abroad, home insurance review, storm damage, energy use via Eliq). Flow described by KBC as "analysis → trigger → solution"; two new cases a month. [KBC newsroom](https://newsroom.kbc.com/kate-five-years-and-five-milestones)
- 6.2M Kate users (group, Q2 2026); 77–82% of Belgian queries solved autonomously; ~400k products sold a year; 13.6% of Kate leads convert (Q3 2025); work of 356–400 FTE. Runs on GPT-4.1 since Oct 2025 ("Kate 2.0").
- KBC principle: "Kate will never implement anything without explicit customer approval."
- Consent: proactive Kate needs **Extra gebruiksgemak**; personalised offers need **Op jouw maat**. [kbc.be FAQ](https://www.kbc.be/particulieren/nl/product/betalen/zelf-bankieren/met-je-smartphone/mobile/kbc-mobile-faqs/communicatie-contact-acties.html)
- Existing features we reuse rather than rebuild: "Saldo lager dan €100" alert, scheduled-payment reminder, subscription overview, "Automatisch sparen – variabel bedrag", "Sparen voor je dromen", volmacht in the app, Bolero portfolio view (trading still in the separate Bolero app), Mijnenergie energy switching.

## Prior art (be honest: the parts exist, the combination is new)

- Commonwealth Bank "Customer Engagement Engine" (Pega): next-best-conversation, ~55M decisions a day; Benefits Finder A$1B+ to customers. No public "do nothing" option.
- DBS: ~S$1B AI/data value in 2025, measured against control groups (includes fraud, credit, productivity).
- Monzo/Eno/Rocket Money (bill changes), Chip/Plum (auto-saving), OpenClaw heartbeat (`HEARTBEAT_OK`, a first-class "nothing to say"), Hermes agent (memory as readable entries).
- Fewer, better notifications work: Pinterest cut volume 6–24% and raised CTR 11–31% (KDD 2018). Overdraft alerts cut overdrafts 9% (INFORMS Management Science).

## Does it make KBC money?

- Moderately positive, if money stays at KBC and trust turns into relationship products. Risks to say out loud: nudging idle cash erodes deposit margin (apps already cut deposit-franchise value 14–22%, NBER); "already insured" cards cost insurance sales; no bank has isolated next-best-action profit.
- KBC's own lesson: the Sept 2023 state note cost KBC ~€5.7bn in deposits and ~€87m of net interest income to win back. Defending deposits early is the biggest lever.
- Illustrative Belgian case (1M users, 2% shown a card per week): net −€7.4m / +€8.4m / +€36m a year (low/base/high). AI tokens are negligible; the case depends on acceptance and deposit retention. Assumptions are marked in the plan.

## Regulation shaping the design

- GDPR: Belgian DPA decision 46/2024 accepted modelling on pseudonymised transaction data under legitimate interest, with opt-in consent for personalised offers and a separate Art. 21 objection. Art. 9: never infer health conditions (we detect "PIN resets", not "dementia").
- EU AI Act: creditworthiness is high-risk (Annex III 5b), so credit and investment situations always hand off to a person; transparency duties apply (label AI).
- MiFID: investing needs a suitability check, hence the Bolero option routes to an advisor.

## Belgian facts used in the demo

- Regulated savings interest tax-free up to €1,020 (2026); term accounts pay 30% withholding tax.
- Last 1-year state note (Sep 2026): 1.93% net. KBC savings: 0.40% base + 0.30% loyalty.
- Health-insurer sports refunds: Helan up to €50/year, CM €15/year. Voetbal Vlaanderen members are insured through the federation.
- Student jobs: 650 hours/year at the 2.71% solidarity contribution.
